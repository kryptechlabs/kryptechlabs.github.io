Family Asset Tracker

Everything a family owns, on one page. Gold, land, deposits, shares and policies, each with an owner, a value and a date.

Screens

Design decisions

Design decisions you can see in the screens.

One number first

The home screen opens on the family total for today, then shows how much of it was invested and how much is gain. A switch moves between the whole family and your own holdings.

Values that stay current

Gold is valued from the live rate for its purity, so a 22K necklace and a 24K coin are priced correctly. The form shows what an item is worth today before you save it.

Totals you can trust

Insurance is listed as cover and kept out of the total, so the headline number is what the family owns and nothing else.

Private by default

Holdings stay on the device and only price lookups go online. Family members have their own profiles and PINs, and the per-person view is for the admin only.

Written for Indian households

Amounts appear in lakh and crore with Indian digit grouping, and the categories include temple jewellery alongside equities and fixed deposits.
