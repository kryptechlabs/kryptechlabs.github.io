1. Who we are

This website is run by KrypTech Labs Private Limited, a company incorporated in India (CIN [CIN: Corporate Identification Number]), with its registered office at [Registered office address] (“KrypTech Labs”, “we”, “us”).

This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it and what you can do about it. It is written to meet India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers personal data you share with us when you visit this website, fill in the contact form, book a call through our booking page, or email or call us.

It does not cover data we handle for clients as part of a project. That is covered by the agreement we sign with each client.

4. How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and answer your questions.
  • To schedule calls and prepare a proposal, scope or quote you asked for.
  • To keep a record of our business correspondence.
  • To protect the website and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, rent it out, or use it for advertising. We will not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

5. Why we are allowed to use it

You give us this data voluntarily so that we can respond to you. We use it only for that purpose and the related purposes listed above. Where the law requires your consent for something else, we will ask for it first, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

6. Who we share it with

We share your data only with service providers that help us run this website and reply to you, and only as much as they need:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail), where we receive and store our email.
  • Cal.com, which runs our booking page. Its own privacy policy applies to the booking page.
  • Our website hosting provider, which delivers the website and protects it from abuse.
  • Specialist partners such as developers, only once you have engaged us for a project, and only under a confidentiality agreement.
  • Government authorities, when the law requires us to.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside India.

7. Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Our booking page is run by Cal.com, which may set its own cookies when you open it.

8. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries that don’t become projects for up to two years after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a client, we keep correspondence for as long as the law requires us to keep business records. You can ask us to delete your data sooner.

9. How we protect it

We limit access to your data to the people who need it to reply to you or work on your project, and we use established service providers to store and deliver it. No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct, complete or update your data.
  • Delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw any consent you have given.
  • Name another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or are unable to.

Write to us at kryptechlabs@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act. We will respond within 90 days, and usually much sooner.
