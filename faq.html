Frequently asked

About KrypTech Labs

What does KrypTech Labs do?

We design and build websites, mobile apps, digital products such as dashboards and portals, and AI tools. Services lists what each one includes.

Who do you work with?

Businesses and growing teams. Our focus is companies that sell to other businesses.

Where are you based?

Our registered office is in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. We work by call, email and shared documents, so you don’t need to be in Coimbatore to work with us.

Who will I be working with?

One person from the first conversation to launch day: Arul K Murthy, who leads research and design on every project. Development and SEO are done by specialist partners we bring in for each project. Meet the team.

Starting a project

How do I start a project?

Send us a message through the contact form, or book a 30-minute call. We’ll ask about your business, your users and your goals, then send you a written scope and price.

What do you need from me to begin?

A short description of what you want to build, and why. Anything you already have helps: an existing website, content, brand files, or examples of things you like.

Will you sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA)?

Yes. We’re happy to sign one before detailed discussions. Please don’t share confidential information through the contact form before that.

Price and timeline

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. After the first conversation we send you a written scope with one price for that scope, agreed before any work starts. SEO is scoped and quoted separately.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We agree the timeline with you in the written scope, and you get clear updates at each of the four steps: research, design, build and launch.

After launch

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We ship it, then stay on to support you. What the support covers is agreed in the written scope.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes. We build websites to be easy to update.
